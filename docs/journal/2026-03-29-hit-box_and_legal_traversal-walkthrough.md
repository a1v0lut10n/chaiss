# Fully Interactive Spatial Hit-Boxes

We have officially transitioned the chess board from a visual array into a fully interactive sandbox driven by rigorous path modeling! 

## What Was Accomplished

1. **Rigorous Move Formatting (`get_legal_moves`)**
   We expanded the engine from simply mapping "Threat Vectors" into mapping physically legal traversal blocks. 
   - **Pawns now properly push:** They natively evaluate distance `+1`/`-1` recursively, and correctly evaluate the `+2`/`-2` double jump from their starting rows natively!
   - **En Passant Capture Maths:** The Pawn capture loops strictly enforce checking our `GameState`'s `pub en_passant_target: Option<Square>`. If an enemy Pawn double-steps through a diagonally held heat sector, your pawn now physically sees that geometric tile as a valid capture destination mathematically!

2. **Native Egui Context Hit-Boxes**
   Instead of wiring up terrifying global mouse-capture traits searching for pixels, we simply fed `egui::Sense::click()` natively into our resizing cell layout!
   - Every square on the grid natively registers intersections dynamically. 
   - When hovering over squares, they illuminate.
   - When you click a square containing an active piece belonging to your turn's `active_color`, the `ChaissApp` naturally captures that `selected_square` integer and highlights the square natively with an amber overlay!

3. **Guidance Overlay & Traversal Execution**
   - Once a square is "Active", we render a subtle black target-reticle natively into the center of every square mathematically generated by `get_legal_moves(..., active_piece)`. You visually see exactly where that piece is physically capable of jumping to. 
   - Clicking a valid target tile fires `apply_move()`! The Engine physically obliterates the piece from the old square, mounts it into the new one, automatically computes the `En Passant` tracking states for any double-pushed pawns dynamically, and toggles the `active_color` turnover seamlessly!

## Play For Yourself
Everything builds cleanly. If you interact with the background `cargo run` window currently active, you can physically lift and place pawns, testing the double pushes cleanly natively without any visual tearing regardless of pixel window sizing!
